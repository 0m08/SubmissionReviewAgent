### 1. Key Anti-Patterns Added

- **The Encyclopedia Trap:** Cramming multiple tools, dimensions, sub-variants, or niche field tricks into a single dense 150-word paragraph just to avoid "losing" generator content. If a detail is not essential to the tool's foundational purpose, cut it.
- **Cold / Unbridged Endings:** Ending a slide on an isolated technical specification or flat declarative statement without teeing up the problem or question that the next slide resolves.
- **Micro-Slicing:** Spreading one single mechanical idea across 4–5 consecutive thin slides (e.g., separate slides for internal burrs, external burrs, failure modes, swarf, and cost of burrs).
- **Summary Itemization:** Summaries that begin with *"We have covered..."* or merely list 15 tool names instead of synthesizing 2–3 overarching professional trade principles under the mandatory `"In this topic, you learned..."` formula.
- **Formatting Creep:** Sneaking markdown bullets, bold headers, or em dashes (`—`) inside slide chunk prose paragraphs.
