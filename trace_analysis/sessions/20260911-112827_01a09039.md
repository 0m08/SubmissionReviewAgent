### Course Overview & Instructional Intent

#### 1. Scope & Subject Matter
This course covers foundational trade equipment across four distinct modules:
- **Topic 01: Common Hand Tools** (Striking, driving, leveling, cutting, shaping, holding, assembling, and hand excavation tools, along with baseline PPE and pre-use inspection).
- **Topic 02: Toolboxes & Bags** (Selecting carriers—hard boxes, open totes, backpacks—and ergonomics, vertical organization, weight distribution, and tool care).
- **Topic 03: Level Basics** (Level vs. plumb, reading spirit vials, measuring slope/pitch, calibration via 180-degree reversal tests, and specialized levels).
- **Topic 04: Hacksaw Blade Selection** (TPI selection, the Three-Teeth Rule, material thickness matching, carbon vs. bi-metal blades, blade orientation, and kerf).

#### 2. Target Audience & Core Intent
- **Audience:** Entry-level field apprentices and junior technicians in the mechanical/building trades (HVAC, plumbing, and electrical).
- **Pedagogical Intent:** The course is designed to build **practical job-site readiness and equipment respect**. It is not an engineering textbook or a parts catalog. The intent is to teach apprentices:
  - How to recognize the right tool for a specific task.
  - How to handle tools properly to avoid personal injury (e.g., mushroomed chisels, exposed blades, underground utility strikes via 811).
  - How to prevent damage to expensive equipment and workpieces (e.g., avoiding cam-out, using backup wrenches on copper tubing, preventing hacksaw blade binding).
