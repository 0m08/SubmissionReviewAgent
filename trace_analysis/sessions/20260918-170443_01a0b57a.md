### Storytelling Flow

The material follows a chronological, on-the-job workflow: **Measure $\rightarrow$ Align $\rightarrow$ Mark & Lay Out**.

1. **Measuring Dimensions (Length & Clearance)**: Begins with establishing raw measurements and fitting allowances, starting with the primary tool (the tape measure) and moving directly to tools that solve its physical limitations.
2. **Establishing Spatial Orientation (Level, Plumb, & Pitch)**: Moves from scalar dimensions to 3D spatial alignment—ensuring lines run horizontally true (with correct drainage slope), vertically plumb, and square at 90-degree intersections.
3. **Translating Layout into Physical Marks**: Concludes by taking those dimensional and alignment references and locking them onto physical surfaces and pipes before making a cut or boring a hole.

---

The narrative connects tools through functional handoffs and job-site trade-offs:

* **Complementary Pairs (Flexible vs. Rigid)**:

  * The **folding rule** is introduced directly as the solution to blade collapse, using its rigidity for vertical reach and its brass slide for exact internal cabinet/rough-in clearances.
